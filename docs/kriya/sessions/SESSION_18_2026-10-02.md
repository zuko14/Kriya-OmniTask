## Decisions made / needed
- **D19 (Payments & Mandate Agent Architecture with Prepayment Slot Holds & Webhook Settlement)**:
  - Payments agent is a charter-based bounded agent loop (autonomy cap `T2`, owner `billing_manager`).
  - Strict code-binding of `customerRef` from conversation session context prevents cross-customer financial manipulation.
  - Prepayment slot reservations follow the `slot_hold` pattern (ADR-011): unexpired holds are atomically deducted from availability and convert to confirmed appointments upon webhook settlement.
  - Cryptographic webhook verifiers support Razorpay, Stripe, and Kriya Pay with `timingSafeEqual`.
  - Over-limit actions breach mandate and park at `human_gate`, routing P1 Attention items to `billing_manager`.
  - Every settlement and authorized refund issues an immutable Ed25519 proof receipt and records Customer 360 timeline events.
