## Decisions made / needed
- **D20 (Connector Framework & External Calendar Synchronization Architecture)**:
  - Connectors are stored in `tenant_connectors` with category (`calendar`, `crm`, `messaging`, `payments`, `erp`), status (`active`, `inactive`, `error`), and `vault_service_slug`.
  - Credentials (client ID, client secret, access token, refresh token, expiry) are stored in `CredentialVault`, NEVER in plain text or directly in connector records.
  - `GoogleCalendarConnector` automatically refreshes expired access tokens via Google OAuth token endpoint and updates the vault; transparently handles 401 by clearing cached tokens and retrying once.
  - External calendar sync adheres to ADR-011: Kriya appointment book is the system of record. External calendars are synchronized atomically; tentative slot holds reserve slots as `[HOLD]` events, and confirmed bookings convert or insert confirmed events.
  - Read-back verification and saga compensation ensure external calendar events are cleaned up if workflow steps fail or are rolled back.
