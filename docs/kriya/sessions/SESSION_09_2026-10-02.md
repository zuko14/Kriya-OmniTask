## Decisions made (D7 closed, D9 decided)
- **D7 (Closed):** Emergency triage (P0_CRITICAL) routes immediately to branch `emergency_role` (default: `'emergency_on_call'`) with zero delay (`after_hours: 0`, `next_available_at: null`), bypassing ordinary branch operating hours.
- **D9 (Decided):** Attention items are deterministically routed to the right human role by branch, operating hours, and priority-ordered condition rules. The Verification agent runs async read-back jobs (`verification_jobs`) against external systems of record, failing closed into the Attention queue on discrepancies or deadline expiration.
